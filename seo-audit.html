Executive Summary

InnerCurrent already has the ingredients of a trustworthy counselling site: Aaron's warmth, PACFA status, Monash qualification, $150 fee, free initial call, online format, crisis disclaimer and clear booking pathway.

The main issue is not trust. It is specificity. The site currently feels broad and reflective, but Google and potential clients need clearer signals about who Aaron helps, what problems he is best suited for, and why someone should book now rather than keep browsing.

Best next positioning Online counselling for adults navigating burnout, overwhelm, life transitions, caring roles and the invisible pressure of holding everything together.

Homepage Rewrite Direction

The homepage should keep its warm, reflective tone, but become more explicit about what Aaron helps with. The first viewport should answer: "Is this for me, and what do I do next?"

H1: Online Counselling for Burnout, Overwhelm and Life Transitions Supporting line: A warm, reflective space for adults who feel stuck, depleted, anxious, or weighed down by what they are carrying. Primary CTA: Book a Free Initial Call Secondary CTA: Explore Counselling Services

Homepage SEO Metadata

The current title and description are warm, but slightly long and broad. Sharpen them around the primary intent: online counselling in Melbourne for burnout, anxiety and life transitions.

Title: Online Counselling Melbourne | Burnout, Anxiety & Life Transitions Meta description: Online counselling for adults navigating burnout, anxiety, overwhelm, self-worth, caring roles and life transitions. Book a free 15-minute call with Aaron Loh.

Services Page Improvements

The Services page is already clear about the free call and $150 session fee. The next step is to reduce uncertainty before someone clicks Book.

  • Add a visible PACFA and qualification trust line near the top.
  • Confirm online-only for now and whether clients can attend Australia-wide.
  • Clarify Medicare, private health and NDIS availability.
  • Add "who this is for" and "who this may not be for".
  • Add a short "what happens after booking" section.
  • Repeat the free initial call CTA after the FAQ.

Technical SEO Notes

  • All checked public pages returned 200 status.
  • The site has a sitemap and robots file.
  • The sitemap lists /home, while the homepage canonical points to the root URL. This is workable, but cleaner if the sitemap promotes the root URL.
  • Canonical tags exist on checked pages.
  • Open Graph image is present, but it appears to use an http image URL. Prefer https if Squarespace allows it.
  • Basic Website schema exists, but the description field is empty.
  • No Google Search Console is set up yet. This is the most important measurement task.

Google Search Console Setup

Set this up before making a big content push so you can track queries, impressions, click-through rates and indexing issues.

  1. Go to Google Search Console.
  2. Add the domain property: innercurrent.com.au.
  3. Verify using DNS if possible, or Squarespace's supported verification method.
  4. Submit the sitemap: https://www.innercurrent.com.au/sitemap.xml.
  5. After 2-4 weeks, export queries and pages for review.
